Abstract

摘要
The illumination technique used in the industrial inspection systems can affect the quality of the acquired images. The choice of this technique plays an important role in the designing process of the system. Using multiple modalities can then avoid a difficult choice and increase the performances. We propose to consider multi-modal data by constructing pseudo-spectral cubes and by using hyperspectral imagery algorithms to detect 3D surface defects on metal parts. In this paper, four basic lighting modalities (white source light and monochromatic source light including non-polarized and polarized light) are proposed and tested to illuminate the metal parts, and different detection algorithms have been compared. We show that the spectral angle mapper is able to detect the defects with a non-supervised approach, and gives good detection performances.
Index Terms—Hyperspectral imaging, Inspection, Lighting, Object detection.
工業檢測系統所採用的照明技術會直接影響影像擷取品質，因此照明方式的選擇是系統設計中的關鍵環節。若能同時運用多種照明模態，便可避開「只能擇一」的兩難，並提升檢測效能。本文提出以多模態資料建構「偽光譜立方體」，再套用高光譜影像演算法，以偵測金屬零件表面的三維缺陷。研究中提出並測試四種基本照明模態（白光與單色光，各分為非偏振與偏振），並比較多種檢測演算法。結果顯示，光譜角映射（SAM）在非監督式設定下即可偵測缺陷，且具有良好的檢測表現。
關鍵詞：高光譜影像、檢測、照明、物件偵測。

I. Introduction

引言
Illumination is a particularly important component of the image acquisition system for both human and automatic surface inspection. The choice of the illumination technique is a crucial step in the designing process of any machine vision system. The goal of illumination in machine vision is to make the important features of the object to be inspected visible and reduce undesired features [1]. The quality of these important features is related to the illumination concept, as they need to be presented with a maximum of contrast. The challenge of illumination is to increase the signal to noise ratio, and to emphasize and explore these features to maximize the contrast. The means to increase the contrast are the direction of the light, the choice of the light spectral band and the effect of polarization [2].
無論是人工目視或自動化表面檢測，照明都是影像擷取系統中格外重要的一環；任何機器視覺系統在設計時，照明技術的選擇都是關鍵步驟。機器視覺中照明的目的，在於讓待測物的重要特徵清楚可見，同時抑制不需要的特徵 [1]。這些特徵能否被良好呈現取決於照明設計，因為它們必須以最大對比度呈現。照明所面對的挑戰，是提高訊雜比，並凸顯這些特徵以使對比度最大化。提升對比度的手段主要有三：光線的入射方向、光譜波段的選擇，以及偏振效應 [2]。

C. Light perception and colors interpretation｜光線感知與色彩判讀
The light perception of machine vision cameras differs from the human eye. Machine vision uses in most cases light with wavelengths between 380 nm and 1100 nm (from blue light to near infrared). To interpret and determine colors, it is necessary to combine the information of three (or four) pixels with different color sensitivity, corresponding to red, green and blue (RGB) for example, or the complementary colors cyan, yellow and magenta, which are typical triples to determine the color values. In practice this is realized with micro-optical mosaic filters for single chip color cameras. These color filters cover the single pixels inside the image sensor with a special pattern (for example the Bayer pattern) and make them spectrally sensitive (one-chip-color camera). Another construction uses three separate sensors. Each sensor is made sensitive for only one color (RGB). The information of all three sensors is correlated and delivers the color information (3-CCD-cameras) [2].
機器視覺相機感知光線的方式與人眼不同，多數情況下使用 380 至 1100 nm 的波長（從藍光到近紅外線）。為了判讀色彩，必須結合三個（或四個）具有不同色彩敏感度的像素資訊，例如紅、綠、藍（RGB），或其互補色青、黃、洋紅，這些都是決定色彩值的典型三元組。實務上，單晶片彩色相機以微光學馬賽克濾鏡實現此功能：濾鏡以特定排列（例如 Bayer 排列）覆蓋感測器上的各個像素，使其具有光譜選擇性。另一種構造則使用三個獨立感測器，各自只對一種顏色敏感，再整合三者資訊得出色彩，即所謂 3-CCD 相機 [2]。

D. Modalities｜照明模態
In order to produce multiple data cubes representing different pseudo-spectra for the same area of interest on the tested metallic parts we have used the acquisition system (Fig. 1) that enables us to realize the four basic lighting modalities:
  • UWL (respectively UML) modality is realized by placing white LED (respectively monochromatic LEDs) for illumination and polarizing filter (analyzer) in the front of the camera which is fixed in a selected angular position.
  • PWL (respectively PML) modality is realized by placing white LED (respectively monochromatic LEDs) for illumination followed by a polarizing filter (polarizer) and by placing another polarizing filter (analyzer) in the front of the camera. The polarizer is fixed in a defined angular position.

III. Algorithms

演算法
In the literature, many algorithms for detection and classification are proposed, applied in multispectral and hyperspectral imagery. In the framework of this work, we have tested and compared supervised and non-supervised methods for defect detection. More often, the task of a detection algorithm is to decide, by means of a statistical hypothesis test, whether a target of interest is present or not in a pixel-under-test with observed pixel vector x [4]. To take a decision, we calculate the scalar y = D(x) and compare y to a threshold η.
文獻中已有許多應用於多光譜與高光譜影像的檢測與分類演算法。本研究測試並比較了監督式與非監督式的缺陷檢測方法。檢測演算法的任務，通常是透過統計假設檢定，判斷某個待測像素（其觀測向量為 x）中是否存在感興趣的目標 [4]。決策方式是計算純量 y = D(x)，再與閾值 η 比較。

A practical question of paramount importance for a detection algorithm user is how to set the threshold to keep the number of detection errors small. The AMF detector is based on following hypothesis test, for full pixel detection
H0 : x → N(0, Γ)     H1 : x → N(s, Γ)
Its expression can be found in [4]
yAMF(x, s) = ‖sT Γ̂−1 x‖2sT Γ̂−1 s   (1)

B. Anomaly Detection Algorithm｜異常檢測演算法
The anomaly detectors are unsupervised algorithms, which do not require any knowledge of the spectral signatures of the target of interest. Anomalies are defined with reference to a model of the background. Background models are developed adaptively using reference data from either a local neighborhood of the test pixel or a large section of the image. Anomalies are defined as observations that deviate in some way from the neighboring clutter background or the image-wide clutter background, respectively [6]. The most common used algorithm is RX, given by
yRX(x) = (x − µ)T Γ̂−1 (x − µ)   (3)
This algorithm has also CFAR property [6].

The Kendall’s τ (TAU) is a measure based on the concordance level between s and x, a given target and test pixels, respectively. TAU expresses the probability of concordance less the probability of discordance between x and s. An empiric estimator of the Kendall’s τ is given by [8]
yTAU(x, s) = 2L(L − 1) Σl=1L−1 Σk=l+1L sign[(xk − xl)(sk − sl)]   (6)
with sign(u) = 1 if u > 0, −1 if u < 0, 0 if u = 0, where (xi, si), i = 1 … L, is a sample of L observations of (x, s). Here, we associate a random variable (RV) to each vector pixel and its components are viewed as observations of this RV. The estimated value of τ belongs to [−1, 1]: τ = 0, if there is no dependent between s and x, τ = −1, if the pixels are discordant and τ = 1, when the pixels are concordant.
Kendall's τ（TAU）是以目標光譜 s 與待測像素 x 之間的一致程度為基礎的量測，表示兩者「一致」的機率減去「不一致」的機率，其經驗估計式如式（6）[8]。在此，每個像素向量被視為一個隨機變數，向量的各分量則是該隨機變數的觀測值。τ 的估計值介於 −1 與 1 之間：兩者無相依性時 τ = 0，完全不一致時 τ = −1，完全一致時 τ = 1。

V. Results and Discussion

結果與討論
The detection results of the used algorithms are provided as detection maps, which represent the values of the scalar y defined in (1) − (6) for each pixel. The comparison between the detection results is done by calculating PFA and PD for all the defects present in each metal part. In order to calculate these probabilities, we normalize the detection maps between [0 - 1] and by varying the detection threshold η between 0 and 1 with a chosen step of 10−3, we calculate PD and PFA for each value of the threshold. Once PD and PFA are calculated, we can generate the ROC curves for each detection map.
各演算法的檢測結果以檢測圖呈現，圖中每個像素的值即式（1）至（6）所定義的純量 y。比較方式是針對每件零件上的所有缺陷計算 PFA 與 PD：先將檢測圖正規化到 0 至 1 之間，再以 0.001 為步長，讓閾值 η 從 0 掃描到 1，逐一計算 PD 與 PFA，進而繪出每張檢測圖的 ROC 曲線。

  • Modalities (1) and (2), with white light illumination and small number of components (resp. 3 and 9), are not performant. The performances are improved with ML modalities, which involve a higher number of components (resp. 12 and 36).
  • Polarization can improve the detection in certain cases, depending on the geometry of the defect and the used algorithm.
  • Modalities (3) and (4) are very close to each other and the choice of the best modality between them is very difficult because it depends on the defect. However, the combination of all modalities (modality 5) can give a good compromise between them and for some cases even the best results.
綜合而言：
  • 模態（1）與（2）使用白光照明，分量數少（分別為 3 與 9），表現不佳；改用分量數較多的單色光模態（分別為 12 與 36）後，表現隨之提升。
  • 偏振在某些情況下可以改善檢測，效果取決於缺陷的幾何形狀與所用的演算法。
  • 模態（3）與（4）的表現非常接近，由於最佳選擇會隨缺陷而變，很難在兩者之間擇一。然而，結合所有模態的模態（5）能在兩者之間取得良好折衷，在部分情況下甚至得到最佳結果。

On the other hand, the computational burden of these algorithms depends directly from the size (N × M × L) of the input cube, where N and M are the spatial dimensions and L the spectral dimension, which is expressed in terms of big O notation for all the algorithms used in this paper as: O(NML), except for TAU which is O(NM(L)2). 另一方面，這些演算法的計算負擔直接取決於輸入立方體的大小 N × M × L，其中 N 與 M 為空間維度，L 為光譜維度。以大 O 符號表示，本文所有演算法的複雜度皆為 O(NML)，唯獨 TAU 為 O(NML²)。這表示這些演算法能隨資料量增加而擴展。

VI. Conclusion

結論
In this paper, a comparison study is presented between different lighting modalities used to illuminate metal parts containing 3D artificial surface defects. These modalities are combined in order to obtain data cubes, which are compared and tested by means of supervised and unsupervised detection algorithms dedicated to hyperspectral imaging applications.
本文針對照射含三維人工表面缺陷之金屬零件的不同照明模態，進行了比較研究。這些模態經組合後形成資料立方體，再以高光譜影像領域的監督式與非監督式檢測演算法進行比較與測試。

論證結構總覽

研究問題

在金屬零件表面三維缺陷檢測中，若將不同照明模態（白光或單色光、偏振或非偏振）下拍攝的影像堆疊成「偽光譜立方體」，能否借用高光譜影像演算法提升檢測效能？哪一種模態與演算法的組合效果最好？

核心論點（一句話版本）

結合多種波長照明所建構的偽光譜立方體，搭配以背景為參考的非監督式光譜角映射（SAM），能以極低的誤報率穩定偵測金屬表面的三維缺陷。

證據鏈

  1. 前提：照明決定影像對比度，而波段與偏振是提升對比的兩種手段（第 I 節）。
  2. 方法：以 2 種光源乘以 2 種偏振條件，再加上全部合併，共建構 5 個分量數為 3 至 60 的偽光譜立方體（第 II、IV 節，表 I）。
  3. 比較：以 6 種演算法、4 種目標，在 GDR = 90% 下比較平均誤報率 AFAR（第 V 節，表 III）。
  4. 演算法層級證據：SAM 的 AFAR 為 0.01% 至 1.32%，其他方法多在 27% 至 76% 之間，RX 僅在小缺陷上表現良好（表 III）。
  5. 模態層級證據：單色光立方體（3、4、5）優於偏振白光（2），偏振白光又優於非偏振白光（1）（圖 3）。
  6. 推論：多波長提供互補資訊，因此提升檢測效能；SAM 不需目標光譜且計算簡單，適合工業應用（第 VI 節）。

讓步與反駁的處理方式

  • 作者先承認 HSI 資料量大、運算慢的缺點，再以「簡單的偽光譜立方體」及複雜度分析作為回應。
  • 對 AMF、ACE 的不佳表現，作者以「目標光譜變異大」與「統計假設不成立」兩點加以解釋，並把這個負面結果轉化為採用非監督方法的理由。
  • 對偏振與模態選擇，作者採取保守措辭（「某些情況下」、「難以擇一」），並以「全部合併的模態（5）作為折衷」來迴避無法明確擇優的問題。

結論

多波長照明能為同一缺陷提供資訊互異的多重表徵，從而提升檢測效能；在各演算法中，以背景為參考的 SAM 表現最佳且最穩定，具備工業應用潛力。

論證最強處與最弱處

最強處：SAM 的優勢幅度大且跨樣本一致

在表 III 中，SAM 在三種性質不同的缺陷上，AFAR 都低於 1.5%，與次佳方法相差一個數量級以上；圖 3 的完整 ROC 曲線也呈現相同趨勢。即使考慮平均方式粗糙、樣本數少等問題，如此大的差距也很難單純由隨機變異造成，因此「SAM 優於其他演算法」是全文最可靠的結論。

最弱處：模態效果與分量數互相混淆

五個立方體的分量數（3、9、12、36、60）與表現排序幾乎完全一致，因此「多波長提供互補資訊」與「單純增加維度」這兩種解釋無法區分，而最終結論恰好依賴前者。實驗設計缺少分量數相同、模態不同的對照組；再加上每類缺陷只有一件人工樣本，且沒有任何統計檢定，模態層級的結論應視為初步觀察，而非已確立的因果關係。
